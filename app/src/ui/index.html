Getting started

  1. Add your community with ＋ Add at the top (its name is the first part of its web address, such as example in example.locals.com).
  2. Sign in to Locals in the Locals window, on Locals' own page. Keepsake never sees your password; it only keeps the signed-in session on this computer, as a browser would.
  3. Click Find all videos, then play any video for a few seconds in the Locals window so that your access token is picked up.
  4. Tick the videos you want, choose a folder and click Download.

      Download tools

      Keepsake uses two free programs to save videos: yt-dlp (downloads) and ffmpeg (joins the pieces). They are fetched from their official GitHub pages the first time and checked against their published checksums.

      Privacy

      You sign in on Locals' own page inside the Locals window; Keepsake never sees or stores your password. The signed-in session and your video token are kept only on this computer. Keepsake sends nothing anywhere except to Locals (as your browser would) and, once, to GitHub to fetch the tools.
