**Keepsake desktop app, beta, for Windows, Mac and Linux.** It does everything the browser extension does, and runs the downloads itself: no script needed.

- Sign in on Locals' own page inside the app, or, if you use Google to sign in, reuse your Firefox sign-in with one click. Keepsake never sees your password; only the session is kept on your computer.
- **Find all videos** on a community's Content page and Feed, with titles and dates. Later, **Check for new videos** finds new uploads in seconds and marks them **NEW**.
- Tick the videos you want and **Download**, with progress, pause/resume, automatic retries, and a clear message if your token has expired.
- yt-dlp and ffmpeg are fetched on first use from their official GitHub pages, and checked against their checksums.
- **Rename old downloads** turns `001 v_….mp4` files into titled names.
- **Updates itself** on Windows and Linux; on Mac it tells you when there's a new version.

**Which file?** Windows: `Keepsake-Setup-….exe`. Mac: `…-mac-arm64.dmg` (Apple silicon) or `…-mac-x64.dmg` (Intel). Linux: `….AppImage`.

The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"* (**More info → Run anyway**), and macOS asks you to allow it in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. See the [app guide](https://github.com/al-elessar/keepsake-locals/blob/main/app/README.md#install).
